Lead the canon

You make up the proposta, one note at a time, and the site sings it back as the risposta: the same notes, moved by the interval of entrance, after the delay. Every note you play is checked against the note the risposta is singing with it. Start with Wait for me and the hand shown, then hide the hand, lengthen the delay and set a beat.

Play on the keys below, or on your computer keyboard: the bottom row of letters (Z to M) is the octave below middle C, the home row (A to J) starts on middle C, the top row (Q to I) on the C above. A MIDI keyboard plugged into the computer works too. On a beat, a bar of clicks counts you in; play on each click.

Press Start, then play your first note.
