Move chords

A warm-up for leading a canon. The site names a canon and the note the risposta is singing. Play every note the proposta may move to next, the four note names under one hand (five at the unison), as fast as you can. Any octave counts, as long as it is on the proposta’s own side of the risposta. See Handshapes for the rule.

Play on the keys below, or on your computer keyboard: the bottom row of letters (Z to M) is the octave below middle C, the home row (A to J) starts on middle C, the top row (Q to I) on the C above. A MIDI keyboard plugged into the computer works too.

Press Start, then play.

Canons you miss, or answer slower than your aim, come up more often; this browser remembers them. The answers come from the rules core (src/taneyev/canon-hands.js), not from Taneyev directly.
