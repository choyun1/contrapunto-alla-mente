Continue the proposta

The opening of one of your own canons from the Notebook and WIP pages is given. Write the rest of the proposta, one note at a time, in the notebook’s own rhythm, while the risposta copies what you write a delay later. Each note is checked twice: against the risposta and CF already sounding under it, and, one delay on, its copy in the risposta against the CF there.

Play on the keys below, or on your computer keyboard: the three rows of letters (Z to M, A to J, Q to I) are three octaves, moved to wherever the proposta sits. The keys follow the key signature, so in F major the B key plays B♭; ficta are not entered here. Backspace takes back your last note.

Pick a canon and press Start.

      Your canon

      Each note

      • okNothing to mark
      • pendingDissonant for now: fine if the next note passes on or resolves by step
      • checkWorth a second look, as the Notebook page marks it: a weak-beat dissonance that is not passing or neighbor, or a voice crossing
      • missAgainst the rules: consecutive perfects, a strong-beat dissonance that is not a suspension, a 7th, tritone or other leap the line may not make

      The hand

      • Thumb on the risposta’s note: the four keys under the hand (five at the unison) are the consonant notes against it, as on the Canon proposta page. On a weak beat you may also pass between them by step.

      The checks come from src/taneyev/canon-continue.js, which uses the same florid rules as the Notebook page (src/taneyev/florid.js and parallels.js). Only the strict part of each canon is drilled, up to where the notebook marks it free or cadencing. Some of the notebook’s own notes are flagged as well; the Notebook page lists the same dissonances and parallels under each example.
