Three-voice shifts

With three voices every pair has its own Jv, each measured against how that pair sounded in the original, and the pairs add up: Jv(upper, lower) = Jv(upper, middle) + Jv(middle, lower). Pick the total shift, Jv(upper, lower), to see every way the middle voice can split it. Click a row to hear that split and see its three charts. Intervals are zero-indexed (octave 7, 10th 9, 12th 11).

Worked examples in the notebook: invertible counterpoint over a cantus, three staves of which two sound at a time, and one proposta with two answers.
